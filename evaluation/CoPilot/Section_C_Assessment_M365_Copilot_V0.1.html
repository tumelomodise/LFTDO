Onto-AIoTA Architecture — Validity

CQ-A1
How well does the architecture integrate ontology, IoT, and AI components into a coherent system?

The Onto-AIoTA demonstrates a high degree of architectural coherence by explicitly positioning the LFTDO ontology as the semantic middleware between raw IoT sensor streams and AI-driven decision processes. The Device and Network layers clearly isolate data acquisition concerns, while the Semantic Layer performs semantic lifting (JSON/MQTT → RDF), reasoning (SWRL), and knowledge persistence in a triple store. This separation of concerns reflects established IoT–Semantic Web reference models while remaining domain-specific.

AI is not treated as a black-box classifier but is decomposed into explainable rule-based inference, allowing traceability from sensor observation to TheftEvent classification. This tight coupling between ontology, rules, and application logic supports both interpretability and extensibility.

Strong validity – well-integrated socio-technical system
CQ-A2
How practical is the architecture for real-world livestock farming scenarios in South Africa?

The architecture is explicitly grounded in South African rural realities: low-power solar devices, LoRaWAN connectivity, and intermittent cellular backhaul. These choices align with infrastructure constraints typical of remote farming regions. The cloud-based Semantic and Application layers enable centralized intelligence without requiring advanced local computing skills from farmers.

Importantly, the Business Layer introduces governance, access control, and policy-driven change management, which is often absent in academic IoT designs but essential for operational deployment.

High practical relevance for the target context
