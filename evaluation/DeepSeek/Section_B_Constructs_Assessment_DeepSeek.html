📋 Key recommendations (to strengthen conceptual design):
1. Add AuditTrailPolicy and ConsentManagementPolicy to Business Layer for POPIA completeness.
2. Introduce LocalBuffering (Device Layer) and OTAUpdateMechanism (Network Layer) for rural resilience.
3. Define ontology versioning strategy (owl:versionInfo, backward compatibility) within Change Management.
4. Align LFTDO with ATOL/AHOL via owl:equivalentClass or skos:closeMatch statements.
5. Add CQ9/CQ10 for socio‑technical audit (participant notifications and validation decisions).
6. Decompose Data Mapper into SchemaMapping, ErrorHandling, SerializationFormat sub‑constructs.
