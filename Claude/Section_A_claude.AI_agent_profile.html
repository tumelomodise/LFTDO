Q1

What is your name and model version?

I am Claude, developed by Anthropic. My specific model version is Claude Sonnet 4.6, part of the Claude 4.6 model family, which currently includes Claude Opus 4.6 and Claude Sonnet 4.6. Claude Sonnet 4.6 is designed as a smart, efficient model optimised for everyday and professional use.

Q2

What is your training knowledge cut-off date?

My reliable knowledge cut-off is end of August 2025. I reason about events and information up to that point with confidence. For information beyond this date, I rely on web search tools to retrieve current and up-to-date data.

Q6

What is your specific function in evaluating designed computer science artefacts such as architectures and ontologies?

In this evaluation context, I serve the following roles when assessing artefacts:

  • Structural Analysis — Reviewing logical consistency, modularity, and layering of software or system architectures.
  • Ontology Evaluation — Assessing ontologies against established criteria: coverage, correctness, consistency, conciseness, and adherence to OWL/RDF/SKOS standards.
  • Competency Question Testing — Evaluating whether an ontology can answer its intended competency questions.
  • Best Practice Alignment — Comparing artefacts against domain standards, design patterns, and published frameworks.
  • Critical Critique — Identifying gaps, redundancies, misclassifications, or scalability concerns.
  • Comparative Assessment — Benchmarking a submitted artefact against similar published works in the literature.
Q7

Can you read and interpret images or diagrams, or are you limited to text-based input only?

Yes — I have full multimodal capability. I can process and interpret images directly alongside text input. Supported visual content includes:

  • Architecture Diagrams — UML, component, deployment, and sequence diagrams.
  • Ontology Visualisations — Class hierarchies, property graphs, and Protégé screenshots.
  • Flowcharts & Data Flow Diagrams — System flows, process maps, and ERDs.
  • Tables, Figures & Annotated Screenshots — Structured data and annotated visuals.
Multimodal vision enabled — images can be uploaded directly for analysis without text transcription.
